About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), supervised by Prof. Chau Yuen. I expect to graduate in December 2026.

My research focuses on stacked intelligent metasurfaces (SIM) and wave-domain signal processing for wideband wireless communications. I study how electromagnetic propagation can implement selected communication operations, connecting physical architectures with system modeling, optimization, and link performance.

Previously, I received my M.Sc. and B.Eng. degrees from Southeast University. During my master's research with Prof. Cheng-Xiang Wang, I worked on AI-based predictive channel modeling for 6G wireless systems.

Current focusSIM-enabled MIMO-OFDM transceivers and wave-domain computing.
01

Research

01 /

Metasurface-assisted wideband communications

Wideband beamforming, multiuser MIMO-OFDM, OFDM with index modulation, and resource trade-offs in SIM-assisted OFDMA systems.

02 /

Wave-domain computing for wireless transceivers

My work lies at the intersection of analog computing, electromagnetic wave-domain computing, and wireless signal processing. I investigate how stacked intelligent metasurfaces use wave propagation and interference to implement selected linear operations for MIMO-OFDM transceivers, including spatial precoding, OFDM modulation, and receive-side processing. This connects physical computing architectures with wideband communication models and link-level performance. Ongoing work also explores AFDM waveform generation and integrated spectral shaping.

03 /

AI-based predictive channel modeling

Space–time–frequency channel prediction, scatterer-density-based models, and scenario-adaptive modeling for 6G wireless communications.
